SUBSPACE PROTOCOLS – SUPPLEMENTAL NOTES (CHAT ADDITIONS)

THEME & VISUAL DIRECTION
- Aesthetic reference: Star Trek-themed presentation paired with a Papers, Please bureaucratic gameplay structure.

PLAYABLE LOCATIONS (ADDITION)
- Shop: Accessible location (alongside the Desk and Quarters) where the player can purchase food rations, desk trinkets, and scanner upgrades.

STORY STRUCTURE & LOOP INTEGRATION
- Multiple Endings: The game features multiple endings, with escaping the station as the primary baseline path.
- Scripted Loop Interruptions: Main story beats and side quests inject specific scripted characters directly into the daily shift queue alongside standard procedurally generated ships.

GAMEPLAY RULES & PROGRESSION
- Daily Rule Escalation: Inspection requirements start simple on Day 1 (basic checks) and progressively introduce new rules, documents, and mechanics on later days (for example, Day 4).
- Slow Warp Residue Printer: The physical warp field residue printout prints slowly, acting as a time-management constraint during a shift.
- Manual vs. Automated Shield Scanning: Checking whether an approaching ship's shields are down is a manual job by default. Players can buy an upgrade from the Shop to automate shield scanning.
- Ship Registry Prefixes: Ships carry inspectable Registry Prefixes (example: prefix 442).
- Kordani Audits: Kordani officials can audit the player's desk/work performance.

QUARTERS: FOOD TIERS & HULL SCANNER SWAY
- Hunger Mechanic: The player's food intake in quarters determines hand steadiness when operating the Hull Scanner during the next shift.
- Hull Scanner States (3 Tiers):
  1. Low (Starvation / Unfed): Scanner sways heavily ("all over the place").
  2. Medium (Default / Nutrient Paste): Slight, manageable sway. Nutrient Paste is cheap and keeps the player alive with no extra perk.
  3. High (High-Tier Rations): Grants a next-day perk where the scanner has zero sway for a few seconds.

DESK TRINKETS & REPUTATION MODIFIERS
- Faction Desk Items: Placing a physical item associated with a specific faction on the work desk where a hailing captain can see it on the viewscreen makes captains from that faction more lenient.
