## Overview & Story Structure
- **Path A Among Multiple Endings:** Escaping [[Void_Gate_3]] to the [[Galactic_Union]] is the primary baseline path (**Path A — *The Extraction*** / Ending: ***Across the Line***) alongside 4 other main paths ([[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]).
- **Protagonist Goal:** As an unnamed human [[Main_Character|Port Officer]] trapped on [[Void_Gate_3]], escape the station and secure passage to the [[Galactic_Union]] (where Pact Scrip has zero value).

## Recruitment, Hidden Trust & Threshold Objectives
- **Recruitment & Handler Contact:** The [[Galactic_Union]] handler recruits the player inside [[Void_Gate_3]]'s customs administration ([[Desk]]) and communicates via the [[Quarters]] Personal Terminal (**Messages** & **Decrypt / Encrypt** tabs).
- **Covert Task Pool & Intel Sales:**
  - Complete a pool of covert tasks—such as smuggling a **Blank Data Slate** (`15 SC` on Deck 4 Exchange) into the [[Desk]] hidden pocket to copy target telemetry, pocketing a physical **Warp Field Residue Strip**, or waving designated operatives through customs ([[GU_Double_Agent_Objectives]]).
  - Selling `KDF-`, `BMC-`, `HBR-`, or flagged Concord/Dopple routes to the **Galactic Union Handler** (`4 PU / 20 SC` encryption fee) yields a **Covert Operational Stipend (`20–30 SC`) + GU `rep_score` (`+2`) + Hidden Double-Agent Trust** ([[Quarters_Management]]).
- **Completion Threshold:** Completing the required threshold of covert tasks (e.g., **3 of 5**) earns safe passage off [[Void_Gate_3]] to the [[Galactic_Union]] ([[_State_Variables]]).

## Ending — *Across the Line*
- On the final shift, a weathered civilian GU cargo runner (`isDecommissioned: true`) docks on the [[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic|Expected Ship List]]. You clear it (`Grant Access`), lock your booth door behind you, and slip aboard.
- **Final Visual Scene:** After undocking, the player watches the ship fly slowly away into open space as [[Void_Gate_3]], the dark dead planet, and the dim rust-red [[Brown_Dwarf_System|Brown Dwarf]] star gradually shrink further and further into the distance.
