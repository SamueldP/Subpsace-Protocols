# Krushvak

## Overview
- **Ship Registration Prefix:** **`KRW-`** (**Krushvak War-Vessel**).
- **Home Frontier & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Gharok** (`Krushvak Warrior Frontier`) — **High-Amplitude Zigzag** (tall, sharp triangular peaks with aggressive saw-free symmetrical points).
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **Mid Traffic** (`~8–15 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -3`, Dominant `>= +5`, Crisis `>= +9`).
- **Concept:** A proud warrior empire (culturally Klingon-inspired in older design docs, while visually **explicitly distinct from Klingons** per [[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]) and former rival/enemy of the [[Galactic_Union]].
- **Post-War Alignment:** Rock-solid, permanently stable post-war ally of the [[Galactic_Union]].

## History & Concord War
*(Preserving confirmed canon override alongside `Document_3.pdf`; see [[Concord_War_Timeline]].)*
- **Pre-War (`Phase 0`):** There were **decades of historical war** between the Krushvak and [[Galactic_Union]], followed by roughly **40 years of tense pre-war cold peace** without open war prior to [[The_Concord]] arriving. `[CONFLICT: See _Conflicts_and_Plot_Holes.md]` *(Note: `Document_3.pdf` Part 2, Section 2 wrote "longstanding rivalries without open war.")*
- **Phase 1 — The False Peace:** [[The_Concord]] emerged unannounced from a wormhole on the outer edge of Krushvak space. Alongside minor tech gifts across the galaxy, the Concord specifically gifted advanced medical tech and cloaking devices (equipped with a Concord-only kill switch) to the Krushvak to isolate them and spark resentment.
- **Phase 2 — The Blitz:** Because Krushvak space sat between the wormhole and GU territory, the Krushvak were struck first when the Concord launched a sudden full-scale invasion—remotely disabling their cloaks while infiltrated Dopples sabotaged infrastructure. To survive, the Krushvak and GU set aside past rivalries; the [[Galactic_Union]] stepped in from behind with unconditional support, forging a permanently stable post-war alliance.
- **Phase 3 — Wormhole Collapse:** When the wormhole was destroyed mid-retreat, some Krushvak, [[Galactic_Union]], and allied ships were tragically trapped on the far side.
