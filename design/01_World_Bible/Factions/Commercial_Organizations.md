# Commercial Organizations (Multi-Species)

*Sources: `Subspace Protocols - Game Design & Lore Document_3.pdf`, `gemini-code-1795085727104.md`, and confirmed user overrides.*

## Overview
- Multi-species commercial organizations allow captains of any race—including Humans and [[Galactic_Union]] species—to frequently appear on non-GU vessels passing through [[Void_Gate_3]].
- Each organization carries its own official ship registration prefix ([[Data_Slate_and_Telemetry]]), home lane/system waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]), `power_score` traffic tier ([[_State_Variables]]), and procedural hull language ([[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]).

---

## 1. Independent Free Traders (`FTV-`)
- **Nature:** A loose, open-registry club for unaligned captains (mercenaries, freelance traders, small cargo liners, etc.) who just need legal papers.
- **Primary Lane & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Caldera Rim** (`Free Trader / Far-Line Lane`) — **Alternating Tall-Short Wave**.
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **High Traffic** (`~18–25 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -4`, Dominant `>= +8`, Crisis `>= +14`).
- **Allowed Ship Formats (All 4):** **Military** (*Mercenary Gunboat*), **Civilian** (*Tramp Runner / Shuttle*), **Cargo / Freighter** (*Modular Tramp Hauler*), **Diplomatic / Explorer** (*Refitted Private Yacht*).
- **Visual Style & Reactivity:** Practical, asymmetrical hulls in weathered slate, burnt orange/rust trim, and `#f59e0b` warm amber engine glow. If [[Commercial_Organizations|Mining Guild (`MNG-`)]] crosses `Dominant` (`>= +8`), `FTV-` Free Traders start arriving with **visible hull scorch marks** complaining about Guild escorts ([[Core_Loop_Mechanics]]).
- **Shared Schematic & Story Tie-Ins:**
  - **[[The_Concord|Hun-Borian Free Vessels (`HBR-`)]]** use the `FTV-` ship schematic (confirmed canon override).
  - **Path E (*Out into the Dark*, [[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]):** Maintaining High Reputation (`>= +6`) with `FTV-` (or `FTC-`) plus **600 SC** secures escape on an outer-rim tramp hauler.

## 2. Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)
- **Prefix:** **`FTC-`** *(canonical override superseding draft `FLT-`)*.
- **Primary Lane & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Caldera Rim** (`Free Trader / Far-Line Lane`) — **Alternating Tall-Short Wave**.
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **Mid Traffic** (`~8–15 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -3`, Dominant `>= +5`, Crisis `>= +9`).
- **Role & History with Void Gate 3:**
  - Deep-space logistics and colony transport corporation specializing in extreme-distance voyages.
  - **Original Builders of VG-3:** Far-Line Transit Corp (alongside frontier colonists) originally built [[Void_Gate_3]] outside [[Galactic_Union]] rules, and the station's upper-deck docking and sensor consoles still run on **legacy FTC software** ([[Desk]], [[Station_Atmosphere_and_Society]]).
  - **Station Default & Foreclosure:** Far-Line Transit Corp is a large company that **only defaulted on [[Void_Gate_3]]** during the Concord War ([[Concord_War_Timeline]]) because commercial traffic dried up, they couldn't afford to keep pumping money into the station, and they were short-sighted—not expecting post-war traffic to go back up. The [[Brengop]] foreclosed on the station and solar system via a loan collateral loophole, leaving human technicians and refugees stranded on the lower decks.
- **Allowed Ship Formats (2):** **Cargo / Freighter** (*Deep-Space Colony Ark*) and **Civilian** (*Long-Range Passenger Sleeper*) in industrial cream/bone, deep space navy accent bands, and `#38bdf8` cold ion blue engine glow.
- **Story Tie-In (Path E — *Out into the Dark*):** High Reputation (`>= +6`) with `FTC-` (or `FTV-`) plus **600 SC** unlocks the Independent Escape ending ([[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]).

## 3. Mining Guild (`MNG-`)
- **Nature:** Heavy industrial resource-extraction syndicate operating heavy ore rigs and armed claim-protection escorts.
- **Primary Belt & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Oruun-4** (`Orun & Mining Guild Belt`) — **Repeating Double-Pulse**.
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **High Traffic** (`~18–25 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -4`, Dominant `>= +8`, Crisis `>= +14`).
- **Allowed Ship Formats (2):** **Cargo / Freighter** (*Industrial Ore Rig*) and **Military / War** (*Armored Claim-Guard Frigate*) in dark charcoal steel/cast iron, sharp construction hazard yellow trim, and `#fb923c` furnace orange engine glow.
- **Diegetic World Reactivity ([[Core_Loop_Mechanics]]):** When `MNG-` crosses `Dominant` (`>= +8`), morning Shift Directives report Mining Guild armed frigates claiming independent asteroid belts, **more armed frigates spawn than ore rigs**, and `FTV-` Free Traders arrive with visible hull scorch marks.

---

## 4. Zenith Interstellar (`ZNI-`)
- **Nature:** High-end interstellar cruise and hospitality line transporting wealthy passengers and luxury supplies.
- **Primary Hub & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Solvane** (`Zenith Interstellar Hub`) — **Stepped Arch Wave**.
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **Mid Traffic** (`~8–15 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -3`, Dominant `>= +5`, Crisis `>= +9`).
- **Allowed Ship Formats (2):** **Diplomatic / Explorer** (*Flagship Starliner / Luxury Cruise*) and **Cargo / Freighter** (*Hospitality & Provision Tender*) in deep burgundy/plum or pearl white with polished gold trim and `#a78bfa` / `#2dd4bf` engine glow.
- **Story Tie-In (Path C — *The Buyout*):** In the [[Brengop]] ending (*The Buyout*), the player boards a Zenith Interstellar luxury liner as a wealthy passenger to escape [[Void_Gate_3]] ([[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]).

## Multi-Buyer Intel Economy Note
- **[[Brengop]] Factors** buy `MNG-`, `FTC-`, and `ZNI-` telemetry (`45–65 SC` + `+2 Brengop rep_score`).
- **Deck 4 Exchange Brokers** buy any non-Pact commercial slate (`FTV-`, `FTC-`, `MNG-`, `ZNI-`) for a flat **`35 SC`** (no major faction `rep_score` change; lower audit heat than selling military slates; see [[Quarters_Management]]).
