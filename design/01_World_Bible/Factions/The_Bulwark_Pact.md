# The Bulwark Pact ("The Pact")

## Nomenclature
- **Canonical Name:** **The Bulwark Pact** (slang: **The Pact**) — confirmed canonical name across the vault.

## Formation & Timeline
- Formed **after** the Concord War as a loose, transactional post-war coalition between the crippled [[Kordani]] (`KDF-`, who shattered the Concord offensive at the cost of crippling their own population) and the wealthy [[Brengop]] (`BMC-`, who acted strictly as non-military war profiteers/arms dealers and had already foreclosed on [[Void_Gate_3]]'s brown dwarf system during Phase 2; see [[Concord_War_Timeline]]).

## Internal Dynamic & Division of Labor
- **Loose & Entitled Alliance:** A purely transactional alliance of convenience; both sides dislike each other, and there is constant friction between [[Kordani]] security priorities and [[Brengop]] financial priorities.
- **Economic & Military Exchange:** The wealthy [[Brengop]] finance the [[Kordani]] rebuild; in exchange, the [[Kordani]] provide warships and military training in case another [[The_Concord|Concord]]-level threat appears.
  - *Visual Ship-Sharing ([[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]):* [[Brengop]] Military ships look very similar to [[Kordani]] angular hull silhouettes (reflecting Pact ship-sharing), widened/blunted by `+2–4px` with bolted armor strips and painted in gross industrial yellow.
- **Station Administration:** On [[Void_Gate_3]], the [[Kordani]] run the military garrison and security administration on the station above, while the subterranean [[Brengop]] own the solar system, colonized the dark mineral planet beneath, run dark administrative rings, and operate Brengop Mercantile Operations / Pact Requisition. Oxidized bronze station trim and heavy doors frame Pact authority.
- **The Chip on Their Shoulder:** Because their late intervention broke the Concord offensive, both sides carry a massive chip on their shoulder, acting with immense superiority and believing the quadrant owes them survival and that [[Void_Gate_3]] and the entire sector are rightfully theirs.

## Gameplay & Story Relevance
- **Internal Power Friction:** Built directly into the **Expected Ship List** (Brengop schedule priority vs. Kordani security enforcement) and **Day 17 (Conflicting Pact Directives)** where Kordani Security and Brengop Mercantile Administration issue directly contradictory morning shift memos, forcing the player to choose which Pact boss to satisfy ([[Daily_Rule_Escalation]]).
- **Faction Divergent Endings ([[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]):** Players can side with Kordani Internal Security (**Path B: *The Iron Collar***) or senior Brengop Factors (**Path C: *The Company Man***), or undermine both via **Path A (*The Extraction*)** or **Path D (*The Hollow Gate*)**.
- **GU Double Agent Target:** Key objectives of the [[Galactic_Union]]'s double-agent mission ([[GU_Double_Agent_Objectives]], [[Double Agent]]) include auditing the true speed and scale of the Pact's military rebuild and gathering proof of Kordani/Brengop double-crosses to fracture the Pact from within.
- **Contraband:** Encrypted Pact corporate/military ledgers appear as contraband ([[Data_Slate_and_Telemetry]], [[Station_Atmosphere_and_Society]]).
