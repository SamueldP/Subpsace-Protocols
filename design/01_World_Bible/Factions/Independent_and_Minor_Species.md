# Independent & Minor Species

## Role in Sector
- Situated along the borders and transit lanes between the major powers ([[Galactic_Union]], [[Krushvak]], [[The_Bulwark_Pact]]).
- Multi-species [[Commercial_Organizations]] operate alongside these independent species so Humans and [[Galactic_Union]] species frequently appear on non-GU vessels.
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** All three independent species (`PRJ-`, `ORN-`, `TLV-`) are in the **Mid Traffic** tier (`~8–15 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -3`, Dominant `>= +5`, Crisis `>= +9`).
- **Multi-Buyer Intel Economy ([[Quarters_Management]]):** **Deck 4 Exchange Brokers** purchase any non-Pact independent slate (`PRJ-`, `TLV-` — and [[Brengop]] Factors purchase `ORN-`).

## Active Independent Species Roster, Biometrics & Visual Specs
*(Prefixes, systems, waveforms, and biometrics from `Subspace Protocols - Game Design & Lore Document_3.pdf` / [[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]; visual specs from [[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]].)*

- **Prunjun (`PRJ-` — Prunjun Salvage Registry):** Pakled-inspired haulers; heavy-set, thick-necked, lumpy scavengers with simple blunt expressions and salvaged gear.
  - *Primary Region & Waveform:* **Caldera Rim** — **Alternating Tall-Short Wave**.
  - *Biometric Profile (Day 6+ Automatic Bio-Scanner):* **Unfiltered Recycled O2 (`1.0G`)**, **Warm (`37°C`)**, elevated industrial grease/heavy-metal trace, erratic vitals.
  - *Portrait:* Wide pear-shaped head in olive-tan or dull grey-pink tones, grease smudge pixels on cheeks, crooked salvaged flight cap, and **oversized asymmetrical welding goggles** pushed up onto the forehead.
  - *Ships (`hack_job` Rigid Amalgam):* Uses no standard ship categories. Weird hack-job amalgamations bolted together from 3 different factions' parts with two mandatory locks: an **always-visible round bubble cockpit dome** and **40k-Ork-style painted red-to-yellow flames**.
  - *Day 2 `"Ghost Route"` Suspect Line ([[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic]]):* *"Sending the bright data-packet now! Very fast, very legal, no need to look too close!"*

- **Orun (`ORN-` — Orun Registry Network):** Heavy-boned, **stone-skinned lithoid bureaucrats and archivists**; featured in the draft side quest [[Orun_Freighter_442]] (clearing an Orun freighter with registration **`ORN-442xxx`**, where `xxx` is randomized in-game, to dock even if its inspection details are invalid).
  - *Primary Region & Waveform:* **Oruun-4** (`Orun & Mining Guild Belt`) — **Repeating Double-Pulse**.
  - *Biometric Profile (Day 6+ Automatic Bio-Scanner):* **Dry, Inert-Gas Mixed O2 (`1.2G`)**, **Cold (`24°C`)**, silicate-dense dermal reading, ultra-slow metabolic pulse.
  - *Portrait:* Strictly angular faceted polygonal skull in calcified granite tones (zero soft ellipses), flat chiseled cheekbones, deeply recessed stoic eyes, and crisp geometric high-collared robes with brass trim.
  - *Ships:* **Cargo / Freighter**, **Civilian**, and **Diplomatic / Explorer** only (**no fighter / military ships**). Practical, massive bulk freighters in granite grey with symmetrical heavy girder spines, **2–4 unmistakable clear/transparent cargo containers** showing colorful stacked crates inside, and zero weapon turrets.
  - *Day 2 `"Ghost Route"` Suspect Line ([[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic]]):* *"Telemetry transmitted. Every checksum is verified to the fourth decimal. You will find no fault."*

- **Tolvak (`TLV-` — Tolvak Transit Line):** Independent **desert reptilian tramp freighter crews and haulers** who periodically shed facial scales.
  - *Primary Region & Waveform:* **Caldera Rim** — **Alternating Tall-Short Wave**.
  - *Biometric Profile (Day 6+ Automatic Bio-Scanner):* **Heated, Humid O2 (`1.0G`)**, **Cold-Blooded (Ambient)**, ambient-matched body heat, airborne shed-scale keratin particulates.
  - *Portrait:* Narrow reptilian head in warm desert terracotta/sand tones (distinct from [[Kordani]] cold blues) with lighter-colored **molting scale patches** along the cheek/jaw when `isMolting: true`.
  - *Ships:* **Civilian** and **Cargo / Freighter** only. Terracotta/sand low, flat-bellied desert-skiff silhouettes with long swept dorsal radiator fins.
  - *Day 2 `"Ghost Route"` Suspect Line ([[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic]]):* *"Protocols sent. Look, my schedule is tight—stamp the slate and open the lane."*
