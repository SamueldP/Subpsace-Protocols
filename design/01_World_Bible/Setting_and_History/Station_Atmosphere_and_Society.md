# Station Atmosphere & Society

## Stranded Humans & Lower-Deck Grudge
- **Social Divide on Void Gate 3:** Thousands of human technicians (from [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]) and war refugees remain trapped on the lower maintenance, engineering, and reactor decks of [[Void_Gate_3]] (including **Deck 4**). Meanwhile, [[The_Bulwark_Pact|the Kordani and Brengop]] drafted a small number of literate humans (including the [[Main_Character]]) into upper-deck [[Desk|Port Officer booths]] (such as **Booth 04**) because the station's docking and sensor consoles run on **legacy FTC software**.
- **How Humans Became Trapped:** During the [[Concord_War_Timeline|Concord War]], commercial ship traffic dried up and war refugees flooded VG-3 for safety. After [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp]] defaulted on station bailout loans and the [[Brengop]] foreclosed on the solar system, steep Brengop fees (for **Berth Lease [`30 SC/night`]**, power [`5 SC/PU`], rations, and tariffs) and bureaucratic exit hurdles kept technicians and refugees trapped—calibrated so legal exit to the [[Galactic_Union]] is mathematically impossible without taking risks (or accumulating `600 SC` for an independent escape).
- **Debt Foreclosure & Lower-Deck Human Escapees:** Missing Berth Lease twice strips a Port Officer's badge and reassigns them permanently to the lower reactor decks ([[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]). Lower-deck human escapees sometimes hide in powered cargo bays of tramp haulers (flagged by Heavy Mass `[UNDER REVIEW: Potential pacing cut]` + Violet Cargo SPS + Positive Bio-Scan; see [[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks]]).
- **Lower-Deck Grudge (Open Suggestion):** The thousands of stranded human workers/refugees stuck in the lower decks resent the rare humans drafted into upper-deck Pact control booths (reflected in the *Kordani Coup* ending, where an elevated Chief Port Overseer is despised as a collaborator by the humans below).

## Company-Town Economy & Deck 4 Exchange
- **Pact Scrip (`SC`):** Company-town scrip issued by the [[Brengop]] with **zero value** inside the post-scarcity [[Galactic_Union]] ([[_State_Variables]]).
- **Two Terminal Markets:** The official **Pact Requisition** ([[Brengop]] Mercantile Consortium) operates alongside **Deck 4 Exchange**—a lower-deck human grey-market barter network and intel broker buying non-Pact slates (`35 SC`) and selling Blank Data Slates (`15 SC`) and Shielded Pocket Linings (`90 SC`) ([[Shop]], [[Quarters_Management]]). Deck 4 salvage is also scavenged to repair upper-deck consoles (e.g., the Day 3 spectrometer coil).

## Two-Headed Bureaucracy & Friction
- Daily administration on [[Void_Gate_3]] features constant conflict between [[Brengop]] schedule/profit priorities and [[Kordani]] security enforcement—seen in the [[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic|Expected Ship List]] double-bind and the **Day 17 (Conflicting Pact Directives)** milestone ([[Daily_Rule_Escalation]]).

## Contraband Trends
- **Dense Inert Contraband:** Undeclared ore, black-market weapons, smuggled military hull armor.
- **Hidden Living Passengers:** Lower-deck human escapees, fleeing [[The_Concord|Hun-Borians]], or smuggled specimens.
- **Low-Mass Powered Hardware / Salvaged [[The_Concord|Concord]] Tech:** Active Concord cloaking coils, illegal medical tech, hidden surveillance relays.
- **Forged [[Galactic_Union]] Citizen Papers:** Carried either by desperate innocent refugees or hiding [[The_Concord|Dopples]].
- **Encrypted [[The_Bulwark_Pact|Pact]] Corporate/Military Ledgers:** Sensitive financial or military records.
