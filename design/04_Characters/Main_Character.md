# Main Character (The Port Officer)

## Identity & Role
- **Identity:** First-person, **unnamed human Port Officer** assigned to **Booth 04** ([[Desk]]) on [[Void_Gate_3]].
- **Occupation:** Works customs and docking control in an upper-deck booth on [[Void_Gate_3]], operating docking and sensor consoles that run on **legacy FTC software** (built by [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]).
- **Fixed Shift Auto-Dialogue:**
  - `"Send subspace protocols."` (requests ship telemetry/manifest packet via subspace onto the [[Data_Slate_and_Telemetry|Data Slate]] once shields and weapons are addressed).
  - `"Confirm route, purpose of visit, and cargo."` (automatically issued once the Data Slate loads — **enabled from Day 1 per confirmed user override**).
- **Primary Goal:** Escape [[Void_Gate_3]] (e.g., via Path A *Across the Line* to the [[Galactic_Union]], Path E *Out into the Dark* via `600 SC` Independent Passage Fund, or Path C *The Buyout*), or rise within [[Void_Gate_3]] via Path B (*The Kordani Coup*) or Path D (*The Quiet Return*) — see [[Endings_and_Story_Paths]].

## Background & Station Standing
- **Drafted into the Upper Decks:** While thousands of human technicians (from [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp]]) and war refugees remain trapped on the lower maintenance, engineering, and reactor decks, the [[Kordani]] and [[Brengop]] drafted a small number of literate humans into the upper-deck Port Officer booths because the station's docking and sensor consoles run on **legacy FTC software** ([[Station_Atmosphere_and_Society]]).
- **How Humans Became Trapped:** During the [[Concord_War_Timeline|Concord War]], refugees flooded VG-3 and Far-Line Transit Corp defaulted on station bailout loans, allowing the [[Brengop]] to foreclose on the solar system. Steep Brengop fees (**Berth Lease `30 SC/night`**, power **`5 SC/PU`**, and exit hurdles) trap the human technicians and refugees on the lower decks, calibrated so legal exit is impossible without taking risks (or accumulating `600 SC` honestly for Path E).
- **Lower-Deck Grudge (Open Suggestion):** The thousands of stranded human workers/refugees stuck in the lower decks resent the rare humans drafted into upper-deck Pact control booths.

## Living Situation
- **Quarters:** Lives alone in [[Quarters]] (no family management; pays `30 SC/night` Berth Lease, manages own rations and `5 SC/PU` terminal power; interacts via point-and-click room objects with no movable player sprite). Missing Berth Lease twice triggers **Debt Foreclosure** (demotion back to the lower reactor decks).
- **Survival:** Hand steadiness on the [[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks|Subspace Power-Grid Scanner (SPS)]] depends on the 3-tier food system (No food 2+ days `0 SC` = Max sway; Nutrient Paste `4 PU / 20 SC` = Minimal sway; High-Tier Rations `6 PU / 30 SC` = No sway; see [[Quarters_Management]], [[_State_Variables]]).
