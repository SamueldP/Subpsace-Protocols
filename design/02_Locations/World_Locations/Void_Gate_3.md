# World Location: Void Gate 3 (VG-3)

## Overview
- **Station Code:** `VG-3` (formerly tested names: *Abyss Station* and *Outer Reach*).
- **Galactic Network:** One of **4 Void Gates** in total across the galaxy.
- **Orbital Position:** Orbits a dark, lightless, mineral-rich planet around a [[Brown_Dwarf_System|Brown Dwarf]] in an isolated expanse with no normal stars nearby.
- **Artificial Stellar Stoker:** Because the brown dwarf lacks natural fusion to anchor a warp jump on its own, Void Gate 3 acts as an artificial [[FTL_and_Stellar_Stoker|Stellar Stoker]], powering and stabilizing the brown dwarf's gravity well so warp-driven ships can lock onto it as a jump anchor.
- **Transit Economics:** While ships **can detour around** Void Gate 3, doing so is **costly**—making VG-3 a highly efficient trade shortcut and much more practical to transit through despite the [[Brengop]]'s steep fees.
- **Physical Condition & Atmosphere ([[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]):** An aging, overcrowded orbital border station now run by [[The_Bulwark_Pact]]. Bulkheads are scuffed, bolted together, stained with hydraulic grease, and wired with exposed ceiling conduits, bathed externally in the [[Brown_Dwarf_System|Brown Dwarf]]'s dim infrared crimson / rust-red glow.

## Origin & Brengop Foreclosure
- **Built by Far-Line Transit Corp:** Originally built independently outside [[Galactic_Union]] rules by a large human engineering consortium (**[[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]**) and frontier colonists.
- **Wartime Default & Foreclosure:** During the [[Concord_War_Timeline|Concord War]], commercial ship traffic dried up as fleets shifted to the front while war refugees flooded the station for safety. Far-Line Transit Corp only defaulted on the station because they could not afford to keep pumping money into it and were short-sighted in assuming traffic would not rebound after the war.
- **Brengop Takeover:** Seeing future post-war value, the [[Brengop]] funneled bailout loans into the consortium on VG-3 and bought mining rights to the dark planet below—using a legal collateral loophole buried in the loan terms to foreclose on the brown dwarf itself and claim ownership of the entire solar system (station, dark planet, outer gas giants, and star).

## Station Zones, Hardware & Administration
- **Upper Decks (Control Booths & Station Defenses):**
  - Customs and docking control booths (**Booth 04** / [[Desk]]), whose docking and sensor consoles run on **legacy FTC software** (human consortium software) and are staffed by a small number of drafted literate humans (including the [[Main_Character]]).
  - **Comms Array Degradation (`Days 1–5` vs. `Day 6+`):** VG-3's comms array runs on aging consortium hardware, and the Brown Dwarf's infrared radiation frequently degrades video signals on Days 1–5; on Day 6, [[Kordani]] Security repairs the station receiver and enforces mandatory visual ID matching ([[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks]]).
  - **Station-Defense Override Weapons:** Late-game manual override unlocked by Kordani Command (`Kordani rep_score >= +8`, Day 14+) allowing Booth 04 to **Destroy** defiant vessels in the viewport ([[Core_Loop_Mechanics]]).
  - [[Kordani]] military garrison and security administration (also enforcing martial law in Path B — *The Kordani Coup*).
  - Dark [[Brengop]] administrative rings ([[Visual_Direction_and_UI_Layout]]).
- **Lower Decks (Including Deck 4 & Reactor Decks):**
  - Maintenance, engineering, and reactor decks housing thousands of stranded human technicians and war refugees under steep [[Brengop]] fees and exit hurdles (**Berth Lease** failures trigger demotion back to the lower reactor decks; see [[Station_Atmosphere_and_Society]]).
  - **Deck 4:** Houses salvaged hardware (such as the replacement spectrometer coil wired into Booth 04 on Day 3) and the **Deck 4 Exchange** grey-market human barter network ([[Shop]], [[Quarters]]).
- **Living & Transition Areas:** [[Quarters]] (hosting **Pact Requisition** & **Deck 4 Exchange** terminal tabs) and the non-interactive `21:9` walk-to-work corridor strip between Quarters and Booth ([[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]).
