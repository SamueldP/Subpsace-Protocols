# Brengop

## Overview
- **Ship Registration Prefix:** **`BMC-`** (**Brengop Mercantile Consortium**).
- **Home Core & Warp Waveform ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **Dul-Krag** (`Brengop Deep-Mine Core`) — **Blocky Square Wave** (flat horizontal tops and bottoms with sharp 90-degree step transitions).
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **High Traffic** (`~18–25 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -4`, Dominant `>= +8`, Crisis `>= +14`).
- **Traits & Background:** Greedy merchants, war profiteers, and information brokers with a subterranean, troll-like evolutionary background.
- **Colonization:** Bought mining rights and colonized the dark, lightless mineral-rich planet orbiting the brown dwarf beneath [[Void_Gate_3]] ([[Brown_Dwarf_System]]), and run dark administrative rings on the station.

## Physical Appearance, Biometrics & Starship Visuals
*(From [[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]] and [[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]])*
- **Portrait Appearance (`64 × 64`):** **Troll-like**, with **little to no pigment** (Pale Putty or Clammy Grey-Pink skin), intentionally gross-looking subterranean dwellers. Wide, squat, lumpy head with heavy sagging lower jowls, heavy low brow ridge overhanging sunken beady eyes, wide bulbous nose, wide downturned mouth, and drooping pointed troll ears.
- **Biometric Profile (Day 6+ Automatic Bio-Scanner):** **Dim, High-CO2 / Mineral-Dust O2 (`1.1G`)**, **Cool (`28°C`)**, subterranean low-light ocular profile, trace heavy-mineral blood iron.
- **Ship Classes & Silhouettes (`120 × 56`):**
  - **Allowed Classes:** Operates **all 4 ship categories** (**Military**, **Civilian**, **Cargo / Freighter**, **Diplomatic / Explorer**).
  - **Visual Design:** Practical, utilitarian hulls in a **gross industrial yellow** (`#423c0e`–`#bdb040`) and grimy brass (`#2e2414`–`#8a6d33`) with dirty orange (`#d97706`) engine glow. Civilian/Cargo/Diplomatic are boxy brick-like barges with bolted ore hoppers; Military imports the [[Kordani]] angular template widened/blunted by `+2–4px` with bolted armor strips (reflecting [[The_Bulwark_Pact|Pact]] ship-sharing).

## Concord War & Foreclosure of Void Gate 3
- **Strictly Non-Military Profiteers:** Stayed strictly non-military during the Concord War, profiting hugely as arms dealers, war profiteers, and information brokers.
- **Station Foreclosure (`Phase 2`):** When war shifted fleets to the front and refugees flooded VG-3, **[[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]** defaulted on the station because they couldn't afford to keep pumping money into it and short-sightedly didn't expect post-war traffic to rebound. The Brengop funneled bailout loans into the station, bought mining rights to the dark planet below, and used a buried collateral loophole to foreclose on the brown dwarf itself and claim ownership of the entire solar system ([[Concord_War_Timeline]]).
- **Post-War Pact:** Formed [[The_Bulwark_Pact]] after the war with the crippled [[Kordani]].
