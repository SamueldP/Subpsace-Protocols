# The Concord (Defeated Invaders)

## Overview
- **Origin:** Extra-sector invaders who emerged unannounced during Phase 1 ([[Concord_War_Timeline]]) from a wormhole on the outer edge of [[Krushvak]] space.
- **Associated War-Graveyard Region ([[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]]):** **The Ashen Rift** (`Restricted Concord War-Graveyard / Ion Nebula`) — **Chaotic Static Spikes** (erratic vertical seismograph spikes and broken gaps; red-flag origin never valid for civilian routes without explicit Pact security clearance).
- **Wartime Strategy:** During the False Peace (Phase 1), they posed as peaceful benefactors, distributed minor tech gifts across the galaxy, specifically gifted advanced medical tech and cloaking devices (equipped with a Concord-only kill switch) to the [[Krushvak]] to isolate them and spark resentment, secretly terraformed a barren planet near the wormhole under cloaks to breed shock troops, and infiltrated governments with Dopples before launching a surprise full-scale invasion in Phase 2.
- **Defeat & Wormhole Collapse:** Driven back by the combined [[Galactic_Union]]–[[Krushvak]] frontline and the late assault by the [[Kordani]]. The wormhole was **destroyed mid-retreat**, stranding some Dopples and Hun-Borians on this side while trapping some GU, Krushvak, and allied ships on the far side.

### The Hun-Borians (`HBR-`)
- **Naming Note:** Changed from *Hung-Borians* to **Hun-Borians**.
- **Ship Registration Prefix:** **`HBR-`** (**Hun-Borian Free Vessel**).
- **Starship Schematic (Confirmed Canon Override):** **Hun-Borian Free Vessels (`HBR-`)** use the **[[Commercial_Organizations|Independent Free Traders (`FTV-`)]] ship schematic** (they are essentially the same).
- **`power_score` Traffic Tier ([[_State_Variables]]):** **Rare Traffic** (`~4–8 ships/run`; Weakened `<= -2`, Dominant `>= +3` [sparks news of stabilizing peaceful Hun-Borian communes, irritating Kordani Security], Crisis `>= +5`).
- **Nature & Conditioning:** Naturally peaceful beings native to a high-gravity planet, giving them immense physical strength and fortitude. The Masters conquered and conditioned them into relentless shock troops; since the war ended, their conditioning is slowly wearing off.
- **Physical Appearance ([[Procedural_Art_Bible_and_Asset_Spec]]):** **Stocky, large creatures** with a massive bull-neck, heavy squared **strong jawlines**, **warm ochre-yellow and rich earth-brown skin** (very different from [[Brengop]] gross yellow), gentle wide-set **pale blue eyes** (`#93c5fd`), and **5–7 short triangular bony spikes on their head instead of hair**.
