# FTL Mechanics & The Artificial Stellar Stoker

## FTL Propulsion & Naming Rules
- **Propulsion:** Ships are self-propelled using **warp fields / warp drives**.
- **Legally Safe Terminology:** "Subspace" and "warp fields / warp drives" (plus "Warp Pylons" in ship geometry) are used; explicitly avoid *Star Trek*-specific phrasing such as *"Warp Factor 9"* or *"Nacelles"* ([[Visual_Direction_and_UI_Layout]]).
- **Gravity-Well Jumping:** Ships jump between the gravity wells of stars.

## Void Gate 3 as an Artificial Stellar Stoker
- **Why a Stoker Is Needed:** The [[Brown_Dwarf_System|Brown Dwarf]] (a failed star glowing in dim infrared crimson) lacks the natural fusion required to anchor a warp jump on its own.
- **Function:** [[Void_Gate_3]] acts as an artificial **Stellar Stoker**—powering and stabilizing the brown dwarf's gravity well so warp-driven ships can lock onto it as a jump anchor.
- **Transit Economics & Detouring (Confirmed Canon Override):** Ships **can detour around [[Void_Gate_3]]**, but doing so is **costly**. Passing through VG-3 is a highly efficient trade shortcut and much more practical for ships despite the [[Brengop]]'s steep transit fees.
- **Stellar Stoker Mass Calibration (Day 4–5) `[UNDER REVIEW: Potential pacing cut]`:** Because VG-3 artificially stabilizes the Brown Dwarf's gravity well so ships can lock on and jump, the Stoker requires an accurate **Hull Mass (Tonnage)** reading via the analog **Gravity-Strain Gauge** ([[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks]]). Granting access to a mismatched mass destabilizes the Stoker and incurs a [[Kordani]] Security Strike.

## Warp Field Residue & Stellar Stoker Surges
- **Warp Field Residue Printer (Day 3+):** Every star system carries residual warp-field harmonics unique to its stellar type and local nebulae. Repaired on Day 3 using a salvaged Deck 4 coil, the printer outputs a physical analog waveform strip compared against the **8 Sector Star System Reference Waveforms** in [[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]] (`Veridia`, `Korr-Vath`, `Dul-Krag`, `Gharok`, `Oruun-4`, `Caldera Rim`, `Solvane`, and `The Ashen Rift`).
  - *Note on Waveforms:* Patterns mentioned in design docs (e.g., zigzag vs. sine-like patterns) serve as reference archetypes alongside more randomized waveform noise.
