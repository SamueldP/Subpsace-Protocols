# Concord War Timeline

*(Preserving confirmed user canon overrides alongside `Subspace Protocols - Game Design & Lore Document_3.pdf`.)*

## Current Era
- **Setting Point:** **2 to 3 years after the end of the Concord War**.

---

## Phase 0 — Pre-War
- **Galaxy & GU–Krushvak Relations (Confirmed Canon Override Preserved):** There were **decades of historical war** between the [[Krushvak]] and the [[Galactic_Union]], followed by roughly **40 years of tense pre-war cold peace** without open war prior to [[The_Concord]] arriving. `[CONFLICT: See _Conflicts_and_Plot_Holes.md]` *(Note: `Document_3.pdf` Part 2, Section 2 omitted the decades of historical war, writing only "longstanding rivalries without open war.")*
- **Kordani Standing:** The [[Kordani]] maintained a hostile standoff against the entire galaxy because they disliked everyone.
- **Void Gate 3 Origin:** Built independently by a large human engineering consortium (**[[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]**) and frontier colonists looking to profit outside [[Galactic_Union]] rules.

---

## Phase 1 — The False Peace
- **Emergence of the Concord:** [[The_Concord]]—the shapeshifting **Masters ("Dopples")** and conditioned high-gravity **Hun-Borians**—emerged unannounced from a wormhole on the outer edge of [[Krushvak]] space.
- **Deceptive Benevolence:** Presented themselves as peaceful benefactors bringing harmony.
- **Targeted Tech Gifts:** Distributed minor tech gifts across the galaxy while specifically gifting **advanced medical tech** and **cloaking devices (equipped with a Concord-only kill switch)** to the [[Krushvak]] to isolate them and spark resentment.
- **Secret Preparations:**
  - Negotiated for a barren planet near the wormhole and secretly terraformed it under cloaks to breed shock troops.
  - Quietly infiltrated governments with Dopples.

---

## Phase 2 — The Blitz & Brengop Foreclosure
- **The Concord Blitz:** [[The_Concord]] struck the [[Krushvak]] first, remotely disabling their cloaking devices while infiltrated Dopples sabotaged infrastructure.
- **Krushvak–GU Frontline Alliance:** Because Krushvak space sat between the wormhole and GU territory, the [[Galactic_Union]] stepped in from behind to back the Krushvak unconditionally—surprising the Concord and forging a permanently stable post-war alliance.
- **Void Gate 3 Default & Brengop Foreclosure:**
  - Because the war shifted fleets to the front, commercial traffic through [[Void_Gate_3]] dried up while war refugees flooded the station for safety.
  - **[[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]]**—though a large company—only defaulted on [[Void_Gate_3]] because they could not afford to keep pumping money into the station and were short-sighted, assuming traffic would not rebound after the war.
  - Seeing future post-war value, the [[Brengop]] funneled bailout loans into the bankrupt consortium on VG-3 and bought mining rights to the dark planet below—using a legal collateral loophole buried in the loan terms to foreclose on the brown dwarf itself and claim ownership of the entire solar system (station, dark planet, outer gas giants, and star).

---

## Phase 3 — The Turning Point & Wormhole Collapse
- **Kordani Assault:** The [[Kordani]] entered the war late with an arrogant, all-out assault that shattered the Concord offensive at the cost of crippling their own population and destroying most of their warships.
- **Brengop Profiteering:** The [[Brengop]] stayed **strictly non-military**, profiting hugely as arms dealers, war profiteers, and information brokers.
- **Wormhole Destruction & The Ashen Rift:** The wormhole was **destroyed mid-retreat**—stranding some Dopples and Hun-Borians on this side while tragically trapping some [[Galactic_Union]], [[Krushvak]], and allied ships on the far side. (**The Ashen Rift** serves as a restricted Concord war-graveyard / ion nebula; see [[Desk_Reference_Manual_Data]].)
- **Post-War Pact:** After the war, the crippled [[Kordani]] and wealthy [[Brengop]] formed [[The_Bulwark_Pact]] (slang: *The Pact*) to rebuild.
