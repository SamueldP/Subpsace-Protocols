# Requisition Terminals (Pact Requisition & Deck 4 Exchange)

## Replacement of Generic "Shop"
- **Two Distinct Terminal Tabs ([[Quarters]] Personal Terminal):** The original single generic shop concept is permanently replaced by two distinct tabs on your [[Quarters]] Personal Computer Terminal ([[Quarters_Management]]):
  1. **Pact Requisition (Official):** Run by the [[Brengop]] Mercantile Consortium for official station consumables, rations, and administrative deferrals.
  2. **Deck 4 Exchange (Grey Market):** A lower-deck human barter network for **Blank Data Slates**, contraband workarounds, espionage items, and unofficial goods (also acts as a non-Pact intel broker in the Multi-Buyer Intel Economy).
- **Power Cost:** Browsing and purchasing on both tabs consumes **0 PU** (only **Decrypt**, **Encrypt & Send Data**, and **Scrub Security Logs** consume Power Units on the Personal Terminal).

---

## Design Rule: NO Mid-Game Permanent Upgrades
- **Confirmed Design Rule:** Permanent hardware upgrades that speed up scanners, auto-highlight prefixes, or speed up the printer are **intentionally excluded** *(superseding earlier open suggestions such as "Faster Thermal Printers" and automated shield scanners)*. The friction, rhythm, and satisfaction of manually performing every check yourself is the core of the game.

### 1. Food Rations (3-Tier SPS Sway System)
- **No Food (`0 PU — 0 SC`):** Triggers **Max sway** on the [[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks|Subspace Power-Grid Scanner (SPS)]] after **2+ days without food**.
- **Nutrient Paste (`4 PU — 20 SC` — Pact Requisition / Food Replicator):** Minimal SPS sway; baseline nutrition.
- **High-Tier Rations (`6 PU — 30 SC` — Pact Requisition / Food Replicator):** **No sway** on the SPS during the next shift *(does NOT grant artificial speed boosts and has ZERO effect on machine/printer speeds)*.

### 2. Approved Consumables & Espionage Sinks
- **Blank [[Data_Slate_and_Telemetry|Data Slate]] (`15 SC` — Deck 4 Exchange):** Snuck into the [[Desk]] via your hidden contraband pocket to copy target ship telemetry during a shift (for side quests or Multi-Buyer Intel sales).
- **Shielded Pocket Lining (`90 SC` — Deck 4 Exchange):** Optional espionage item—does not ease inspection checks; purely conceals **1 smuggled item** in your desk contraband slot during [[Kordani]] 3-Strike sweeps.
- **Brengop "Audit Deferral" Fee (`50 SC` — Pact Requisition):** Legalized bribe to expunge **1 [[Kordani]] Security Strike** from your record.
- **Independent Passage Fund (`600 SC`):** Savings target to buy your own quiet berth off [[Void_Gate_3]] without owing favors to any major power (**Path E — *Out into the Dark***; [[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]).
