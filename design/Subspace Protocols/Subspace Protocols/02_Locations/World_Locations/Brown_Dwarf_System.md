# World Location: The Brown Dwarf System & Sector Geography

## The Void Gate 3 Solar System
- **The Brown Dwarf:** A failed star glowing in dim infrared crimson in an isolated expanse with no normal stars nearby. Because it lacks natural fusion to anchor a warp jump on its own, [[Void_Gate_3]] acts as an artificial [[FTL_and_Stellar_Stoker|Stellar Stoker]] to power and stabilize its gravity well.
  - *Diegetic 4m 30s Shift Timer:* Shift time is tracked visually in the external viewport as the dim, rust-red Brown Dwarf arcs across the sky from shift start to shift end, sinking toward the dark planet's horizon / station superstructure over **4 minutes 30 seconds (`270s`)** ([[Core_Loop_Mechanics]], [[Desk]]).
  - *Environmental Interference:* The Brown Dwarf's infrared radiation causes thermal coil burnouts (Day 3 Shift Directive) and frequently degrades video signals on VG-3's aging consortium comms array during Days 1–5 ([[Inspection_Tools_and_Checks]]).
  - *Legal Significance:* During the [[Concord_War_Timeline|Concord War]], when [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp]] defaulted on VG-3 bailout loans, the [[Brengop]] used a collateral loophole buried in the loan terms to foreclose on the brown dwarf itself—claiming ownership of the entire solar system (station, dark planet, outer gas giants, and star).
- **The Dark Mineral-Rich Planet:** A dark, lightless, mineral-rich planet orbiting the brown dwarf beneath [[Void_Gate_3]]. Because the [[Brengop]] evolved in dark, troll-like subterranean environments, they bought mining rights and colonized/mined this planet.
- **Outer Gas Giants:** Located in the outer system alongside VG-3, the dark mineral-rich planet, and the brown dwarf.

- **Earth & Obulon Nebula *(Earlier Note — `[CONFLICT: See _Conflicts_and_Plot_Holes.md]`)*:** `Subpsace protocols info.pdf` listed Earth (requiring warp residue of the Obulon Nebula), which does not appear in the 8-system Day 3+ reference chart.
- **The Wormhole & Terraformed Planet:** Located on the outer edge of [[Krushvak]] space, from which [[The_Concord]] emerged in Phase 1 ([[Concord_War_Timeline]]). In Phase 3, the wormhole was **destroyed mid-retreat**, stranding some Dopples and Hun-Borians on this side while trapping some [[Galactic_Union]], [[Krushvak]], and allied ships on the far side.
- **Hun-Borian Homeworld:** An unnamed high-gravity (`2.5G+`) planet native to the [[The_Concord|Hun-Borians]].
