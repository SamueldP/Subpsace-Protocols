## Five Main Storyline Paths & Endings

### Path A — The Galactic Union ("The Extraction")
*(See also [[Double Agent]] and [[GU_Double_Agent_Objectives]].)*
- **Arc:** Complete the required threshold (e.g., **3 of 5**) of covert double-agent tasks for your [[Galactic_Union]] handler using the [[Desk]] contraband pocket and nighttime terminal encryption ([[Quarters_Management]], [[_State_Variables]]).
- **Ending — *Across the Line*:** On the final shift, a weathered civilian GU cargo runner (`isDecommissioned: true`) docks on the [[Expected_Ship_List_and_Traffic|Expected Ship List]]. You clear it (`Grant Access`), lock your booth door behind you, and slip aboard.
- **Final Visual Scene:** After undocking, the player watches the ship fly slowly away into open space as [[Void_Gate_3]], the dark dead planet, and the dim rust-red [[Brown_Dwarf_System|Brown Dwarf]] star gradually shrink further and further into the distance.

- **Arc:** Side with senior [[Brengop]] Factors to prioritize corporate profit and undercut [[Kordani]] oversight.

### Path D — The Masters / Dopples ("The Hollow Gate" — Hidden Dark Path)
- **Arc:** Quietly aid stranded [[The_Concord|Concord Masters ("Dopples")]] in hollowing out [[Void_Gate_3]]'s leadership from within.

### Path E — The Independent Escape ("Out into the Dark")
- **Arc:** Refuse all four major powers; accumulate **600 Pact Scrip (`SC`)** honestly (**Independent Passage Fund**) while maintaining **High Reputation (`>= +6`)** with [[Commercial_Organizations|Independent Free Traders (`FTV-`)]] or [[Commercial_Organizations|Far-Line Transit Corp (`FTC-`)]] ([[_State_Variables]]).
- **Ending — *Out into the Dark*:** A captain you treated fairly offers you a quiet berth on an outer-rim tramp hauler. You never reach the utopian [[Galactic_Union]], leaving with almost nothing—but escaping [[Void_Gate_3]] with a clean conscience.
