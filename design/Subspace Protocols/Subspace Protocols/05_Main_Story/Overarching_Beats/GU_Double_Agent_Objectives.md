# Galactic Union Double Agent Objectives (Overarching Beats)

## Why the Galactic Union Wants a Double Agent on Void Gate 3
1. **Audit Pact Military Rebuild:** Auditing the true speed and scale of [[The_Bulwark_Pact]]'s military rebuild (`KDF-` and `BMC-` intel).
2. **Trace Concord Remnants:** Tracing hidden [[The_Concord|Dopples]] and stranded [[The_Concord|Concord / Hun-Borian (`HBR-`)]] remnants using [[Void_Gate_3]] as a quiet hub (plus flagged Concord-route intel).
3. **Fracture the Pact:** Gathering proof of [[Kordani]] / [[Brengop]] double-crosses to fracture [[The_Bulwark_Pact|the Pact]] from within.
4. **Track Black-Market Concord Tech:** Tracking black-market salvaged [[The_Concord|Concord]] tech (medical devices, active cloaking coils, hidden surveillance relays).
5. **Investigate Trapped Far-Side Ships:** Investigating salvage, logs, or beacons from the [[Galactic_Union]] and [[Krushvak]] ships trapped on the far side of the collapsed wormhole ([[Concord_War_Timeline]]).

- Main story beats inject specific [[Scripted_Queue_Characters|scripted characters]] directly into the daily shift queue at the [[Desk]] alongside standard procedurally generated ships.
- Covert extraction and delivery tie into the [[Quarters_Management|Multi-Buyer Intel Economy & 3-Phase Side Quest Operational Flow]]:
  - Buy a **Blank Data Slate (`15 SC`)** on **Deck 4 Exchange** → smuggle via the [[Desk]] hidden pocket (concealable with **Shielded Pocket Lining [`90 SC`]**) → copy target telemetry or pocket a **Warp Field Residue Strip** → pay **`4 PU (20 SC)`** to encrypt and sell to the **Galactic Union Handler** (earning **`20–30 SC`**, **`+2 GU rep_score`**, and **Hidden Double-Agent Trust**) → pay **`5 PU (25 SC)`** to **Scrub Security Logs** before a 3-Strike Kordani audit.
- Counts toward the **[[Double Agent]]** (*Path A — Across the Line*) covert task threshold (e.g., **3 of 5**; see [[Endings_and_Story_Paths]]).
