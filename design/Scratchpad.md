## Quarters
- Personal Terminal
	- Uses more power if you want to decrypt messages, or encrypt
- Charger
	- You can charge items overnight but it costs more power
- Food
	- If you are hungry your hand is not steady when scanning
	- Nutrient paste is cheap, keeps you alive but no benefit
	- High-Tier Rations, give you perks next day
		- Steadier hand when scanning

## Desk Job
- Hull Scanner has 3 States
	- Low - Scanner is all over the place  due to starvation
	- Medium - Default, slight sway but not too bad
	- High - Good food, no sway for a few seconds 
- Warp residue prints out on a slow printer
- Each tool has to be charged, the more you have the faster you are but the more power you use, each use of a tool takes one power charge
- Scanning for shields is a manual job, but you can buy an upgrade to do it automatically 
- If you have a [[Kordani]] military insignia on your [[Desk]] you will get a extra payout if a Kordani audits you, and you will get a hidden stat
	- You can buy that from the [[Shop]]

## Hidden faction counter
- People talk, if you let certain races through but not other your reputation will falter, make wise decisions
- If you have an item from a certain faction on your table and a hailing captain sees it, you will they will be more lenient
